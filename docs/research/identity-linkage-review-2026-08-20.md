## Staged adjudication outcome

An initial downstream complaint led to priority evidence curation. The completed workflow
separated evidence collection from the final decision and used lightweight evidence agents,
independent adjudicators, and a collision/complement audit:

1. lightweight evidence agents extracted candidate occurrences and primary-source evidence;
2. independent adjudication agents classified each proposed link as confirm, hold, or split;
3. the primary reviewer checked the evidence threshold and selected occurrence-level decisions;
4. a collision/complement audit checked for competing same-name candidates and uncovered gaps;
5. every decision was added to persistent link history, while only confirmations populated the
   modelling `person_id`.

The prior current-code baseline was 198 assertions covering 589 occurrence pins, with 1,111
unresolved Candidacies. The exact-scope batch independently accepted 179 occurrence links
across 173 existing People: 62 federal MP, 54 Ontario/trustee, and 63 municipal. Every accepted
link is active proposed-to-one-active-Person, with exact matching body, office, district, and party
context; the audit found no collisions. That stage produced 371 assertions, 768 unique pins, and
932 remaining proposals.

The follow-up pass adjudicated the complete 932-row remainder rather than promoting exact-name
matches in bulk. Three lightweight evidence passes were crossed with independent reviewers, then
reconciled against the active registry and checked for same-event collisions. A subsequent
council-history correction located explicit candidate-history bridges for 34 initially unresolved
occurrences and independently re-adjudicated all 34 as confirmed. A separate review of 12
outcome-consequential council candidacies then confirmed nine more links from explicit
candidate-history evidence. A targeted follow-up found explicit bridges for the remaining three
cases. The full case matrix and
sources are in [the consequential council review](consequential-12-council-identity-review-2026-08-20.md).
