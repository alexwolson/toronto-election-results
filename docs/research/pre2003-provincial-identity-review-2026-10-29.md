# Pre-2003 provincial identity review — 2026-10-29

Scope: the 28 proposed Candidacy-Person links created when the 1995, 1996, 1997, 1999 and 2001 Elections Ontario results were added (ADR 0010). Each packet was adjudicated by two independent reviewers working blind against the decision standard below; a link is confirmed only where both confirmed. The two reviewers agreed on all 28.

Decision standard: confirm on a person-specific source (legislature or Parliament biography, cited Wikipedia biography, news, party list) or on strong structural continuity (same body and office, same or successor district, same party, short gap, uncommon name, no competing same-name candidate). Exact name alone is never enough. Name-compiled aggregators (canadianelectionsdatabase.ca) are supporting only.

## Second pass

The 15 unresolved links went to a second pair of blind reviewers per batch, given the first-pass
notes and directed at person-specific evidence. Three changed to confirmed:

| Candidacy | Name | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1999 Etobicoke North | Diane Johnston | Both reviewers confirmed. The 1995 results label her "Ind Renewal"; the Ontario Renewal Party was the Marxist-Leninist party under another name, led by Johnston, and she is on the 1999 Independent Marxist-Leninist list. |
| 1999 Don Valley East | Fernand Deschamps | Reviewers split; the primary reviewer confirmed on the same party-list bridge (Ind Renewal in Eglinton in 1995, the 1999 Marxist-Leninist list in Don Valley East). The ridings do not overlap. |
| 1996 York South by-election | George Dance | Reviewers split; the primary reviewer confirmed. A biography of George J. Dance says he contested the 1996 York South by-election as Ontario Libertarian leader; he is the Libertarian candidate in Scarborough West in 1995. The biography is on a fan wiki, so this is the weakest of the three. |

The other 12 remain unresolved: Bob Hyman, Dan Largy, Erica Kindl, Geraldine Jackson, Rina Morra,
Shelly Lipsey (twice), Don Roebuck, Marilyn Pepper, Linda Martin, Ron Robins and Dan King. Wikipedia
has no Natural Law Party of Ontario candidate pages for 1995 or 1999 (they survive only on
mirrors), which is where most of these would be settled.

## Third pass

The 12 remaining links went to a third pair of blind reviewers per batch, directed at later and
earlier candidate pages, municipal runs, news, obituaries and party archives. One changed:

| Candidacy | Name | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1999 Scarborough Centre | Rina Morra | Reviewers split; the primary reviewer confirmed. The Interim's 2007 profile says she has run for the Family Coalition Party several times since its founding; every Rina Morra ballot from 1995 to 2007 is FCP in a Toronto riding. The profile does not name the 1995 or 1999 ridings. |

Final count for the 28 proposals: 17 confirmed, 11 unresolved. The 11 are Bob Hyman, Dan Largy,
Erica Kindl, Geraldine Jackson, Shelly Lipsey (twice), Don Roebuck, Marilyn Pepper, Linda Martin,
Ron Robins and Dan King. Elections Canada's 1997 and 2000 results give occupations for several of
them, but Elections Ontario's results carry none, so occupation cannot bridge the provincial runs.

Rina Morra's 2003, 2005 and 2007 candidacies sat on a separate Person record (2003 confirmed,
2005 and 2007 unresolved). On the same evidence the two records are merged by the curated
assertion `rina-morra-family-coalition-ontario-continuity`, and the 2005 and 2007 dispositions
are now confirmed. All five candidacies, 1995 to 2007, resolve to one Person.
