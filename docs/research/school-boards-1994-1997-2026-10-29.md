# 1994 and 1997 school trustee results — 2026-10-29

Scope: the school trustee Contests of the 14 November 1994 and 10 November 1997 municipal
elections (ADR 0010), and the eight 1994 council Contests the City's archive does not report.

## Sources

- City of Toronto, archived "Previous election results" pages for 1994 and 1997
  (`data/reference/municipal_archive/results_1994.pdf`, `results_1997.pdf`; Wayback Machine
  captures of 2004-02-05). Their school-board sections are incomplete. The 1994 page reports the
  Toronto, Scarborough, York and East York boards of education in full, but says "Results not
  available" for Etobicoke and has no North York board section. It lists the Metropolitan Separate
  School Board (MSSB) by municipality, without ward labels for North York or Etobicoke, and gives
  the two French-language bodies only as municipal subtotals. The 1997 page reports most District
  School Board wards under garbled or duplicated labels, gives Ward 6 (the "Ward 27" block) only in
  part, and omits Ward 7.
- The Toronto Star's printed results, read as page images in ProQuest Historical Newspapers:
  - 16 November 1994, page A8, "School Board – Final results" (public boards) and "Municipal
    Council – Final results": <https://www.proquest.com/docview/1355150144>;
  - 16 November 1994, page A9, the continuation (City of York Wards 6–8, the MSSB, and both French
    bodies): <https://www.proquest.com/docview/1355150358>;
  - 11 November 1997, page B11, "School Board Results", with incomplete poll counts:
    <https://www.proquest.com/docview/1347261598>;
  - 14 November 1997, page F5, "School Board Results", every Toronto ward marked COMPLETE:
    <https://www.proquest.com/docview/1347261804>.
  The pages mark winners (an "x" in 1994, bold type in 1997) and print "2 to be elected" for
  two-member areas. The transcription is in `data/reference/star_school_board_pages_1994_1997.csv`.

## Method

`data/reference/school_trustees_1994_1997.csv` holds one row per Candidacy, and each row cites the
source its votes come from in `source_authority`, `source_detail` and `source_locator`.

- 1994: the archive's votes wherever it reports the Contest's candidates completely (Toronto,
  Scarborough, York and East York boards; the MSSB outside Etobicoke; the French council's Toronto
  area). Elsewhere the Star's votes (Etobicoke and North York boards, MSSB Wards 7–9, and the other
  French areas). Ward labels and seat counts come from the Star; Toronto Board of Education wards
  keep the archive's board numbering (Wards 1–13, each naming its city wards).
- 1997: the Star's 14 November page, which is complete, except where it is contradicted by both the
  archive and its own 11 November count (TCDSB Wards 7 and 12) and for TDSB Ward 2, which the Star
  printed without votes ("Due to a ballot problem, results will not be counted until November 15");
  those three Contests take the archive's votes.
- Winners follow the vote totals and the seat counts, and agree with the Star's markers.

Result: 503 trustee Candidacies in 129 Contests (350 in 91 Contests for 1994, 153 in 38 for 1997),
plus 13 council Candidacies that replace the eight 1994 `source_missing` placeholders.

## Seat counts

- Toronto Board of Education 1994: Wards 5 (city wards 5 and 6), 7 (city ward 8), 10 (city wards
  11 and 12), 11 (city wards 13 and 14) and 12 (city ward 15) elected two trustees, as the Star
  prints; the other eight wards elected one.
- East York Board of Education 1994: two trustees per ward. The Star's tables mark one winner per
  ward, but its election-night report says "Two trustees were to be elected in Ward 2", and the
  second-place finishers in Wards 1, 2 and 4 (Gail Nyberg, Elizabeth Rowley, Jane Pitfield) served
  on the 1994–97 board ([East York Board of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_York_Board_of_Education),
  [Elizabeth Rowley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Rowley)).
- Metro Toronto French Language School Council 1994: North York and Scarborough–East York elected
  two each and Toronto three ("2 to be elected", "3 to be elected").
- Every other 1994 and 1997 trustee area elected one.

## Discrepancies

- The Star's 1994 Toronto figures were printed before every poll reported ("technical problems
  have delayed complete figures in some areas"); the archive's totals are higher in every Toronto
  ward and are used. Winners are the same.
- Small differences between the archive and the Star in York, Scarborough Ward 13 and MSSB Wards
  12, 14, 18 and 19 do not change any winner; the archive's figures are used.
- 1994 French-language bodies: the archive gives municipal subtotals, and the Star's area totals do
  not reconcile with them. The Star's Scarborough–East York figures equal the archive's Scarborough
  figures less its East York figures for both bodies, and its North York council figures are almost
  exactly double the archive's. The Star's figures are published and the Contests are `partial`,
  except the Toronto council area (archive, complete) and the acclaimed Etobicoke–North York
  separate area. Winners agree in every source.
- 1997: the Star's 14 November figures for TCDSB Ward 7 (Mike Del Grande 4,930) and Ward 12 (Dave
  Hogg 3,911, ahead of Lisa Romano-Dwyer 3,818) are lower than its own 11 November count with fewer
  polls in, and the archive agrees with the earlier count (Del Grande 7,361; Romano-Dwyer 5,853,
  Hogg 4,969), so the archive's figures are used and Romano-Dwyer is the Ward 12 winner. The
  11 November page had TCDSB Ward 5 reversed (Lucy Kopach ahead); the 14 November page and the
  archive both have Donald Clune elected. In TCDSB Ward 3 the Star prints Fabio Morresi at 2,216
  on both days and the archive at 2,261; the Star's figure is used.
- 1997 French boards: Viamonde Region 1B (Evelyne Chelli, acclaimed) also covers York Region; it is
  included as the City reported it. MonAvenir Metro 2 is `partial`: the Star (marked unofficial)
  gives Claude Reno D'Aigle 1,416 and Francois R. Casas 558, the archive 929 and 379.
- 1994 Metro Council, Don Parkway (unchanged): the archive gives Darlene Scott 4,192; the Star's
  4,912 is consistent with its printed percentage. The archive's figure stands.

## 1994 council Contests filled from the Star

Page A8 reports the five Metro wards the archive omits, all acclaimed (Dennis Flynn, Kingsway
Humber; Dick O'Brien, Markland Centennial; Howard Moscoe, North York Spadina; Bev Salmon, North
York Centre South; Norm Gardner, North York Centre), and North York council Wards 3 (Peter Li Preti,
4,662 votes, elected), 4 (Frank Di Giorgio, acclaimed) and 5 (Maria Rizzo, 5,908, elected). The rows
are in `data/reference/star_1994_council_supplement.csv`; no 1994 Contest is `source_missing` now.
