# The 2010–2017 municipal backlog (2026-10-31)

This pass covered the 112 unresolved council, mayor and trustee proposals from the 2010 and 2014 general elections and the 2012, 2016 and 2017 by-elections. The standard is the one used throughout: a person-specific source, or strong structural continuity for the same office type under an uncommon name; a link across office types needs a person-specific source. A newspaper description counts as person-specific when it names the earlier run or gives a distinctive occupation, role or platform that matches the other run.

All dispositions below are under reviewer `backlog-2010-2017-2026-10-31`. ProQuest links are to the Toronto Star on ProQuest (University of Toronto access).

## Sources

- **Wikipedia election pages.** The candidate notes on the 2010 and 2014 municipal and mayoral election pages were read in full in a browser. WebFetch truncates quotes at about 125 characters and missed notes held in collapsed tables, which is how two first-pass quotes (Gallezot, Sawision) came back cut short and how the 2014 mayoral notes on Himy Syed and Michael Nicula were first missed.
- **The Toronto Star's 9 November 2006 candidate profiles**, which ProQuest holds as full text as well as page images: council ([439095543](https://www.proquest.com/docview/439095543), [439102538](https://www.proquest.com/docview/439102538), [439099227](https://www.proquest.com/docview/439099227), [439099188](https://www.proquest.com/docview/439099188)) and trustees ([439095453](https://www.proquest.com/docview/439095453)). Many targets in this backlog are 2006 runs, and these profiles give their occupations and platforms.
- **Later Star coverage**: the 2010 and 2014 council endorsements, the Catholic board endorsement of 2010, ward race stories, by-election previews and the 2014 profiles of minor mayoral candidates.
- Wikipedia biographies, Spacing's 2010 Ward 27 profile, The Local's 2022 Ward 23 guide and a 2015 Canada Newswire release.

## Data issues found

The 2017 Ward 42 by-election contest included three ballot-accounting lines parsed as candidates ("Votes Under", "Ballots Declined", "Ballots (Over Votes) Rejected"), each with its own Person. They raised the field from the 29 candidates in the City's declaration to 32.

The 2011 provincial results for Willowdale gave the NDP's Alexander Brown 297 votes and the Freedom Party's Amy Brown 5,556. Wikipedia's riding page has the reverse (Alexander Brown 5,556, 12.85%; Amy Brown 297), so the two same-surname rows had swapped vote totals.

Both are fixed in a separate change, described in `result-corrections-2026-10-31.md`, which also checks the other same-surname contests.
