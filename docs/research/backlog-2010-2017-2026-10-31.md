# The 2010–2017 municipal backlog (2026-10-31)

This pass searched the 112 unresolved municipal candidacies from 2010 to 2017 for a source naming each candidate's other run. Wikipedia was the most productive source: candidate biographies, and the candidate notes on the 2010 and 2014 municipal and mayoral election pages. Spacing's 2010 profile of the Ward 27 race covered three more candidates.

Twenty-one dispositions were revised from `unresolved` to `confirmed` under reviewer `backlog-2010-2017-2026-10-31`: Jonah Schein, Jane Pitfield, Morley Kells, Frank de Jong, Michael Laxer (2010 and 2014), Chris Tindal, Simon Wookey, Joel Dick, Don Andrews (2010 and 2014), Douglas Campbell, Benjamin Mbaegbu, Ron Singer, Mark State, Sandra Bussin, Kinga Surma, Thomas Gallezot, George Sawision, Sarah Thomson and John Nunziata. Where a source gave vote totals (Andrews, Mbaegbu, State, Bussin, Thomson, Kells, de Jong, Laxer), they were checked against `election_results.csv` and match. Ron Singer's rests on Wikipedia calling his 2010 run his third attempt at Ward 15, which requires the 2003 Ward 15 run to be his. Candidacies without a Person fell from 748 to 727.

## Left unresolved

- Michael Nicula 2014: the 2014 mayoral page names his party as PACT, and other sources tie the Online Party of Canada to him, but no source names both runs.
- Tom Rakocevic 2010 trustee: the Catholic Register lists a candidate of that name in TCDSB Ward 4, but his MPP biographies do not mention a trustee run. His Wikipedia page was not reachable through the fetch tool.
- Robert Spencer, Fil Giannakopoulos, Alonzo Bartley, David Sparrow, Murphy Browne, Mike Yen, Heath Thomas, Himy Syed, Donna Braniff and Arthur Smitherman: sources confirm one of the runs, or place the candidate nearby, but none names both.
- Josh Borenstein: his 2014 provincial run was in York Centre and the council run in Scarborough-Agincourt, so the pairing is doubtful.

The 2016 and 2017 by-elections and most 2014 trustee races had little coverage, and none of their rows were resolved.
