# Endorsement backfill discovery: Toronto mayor and councillor contests, 2003–2014

**Research date:** 2026-08-21  
**Scope:** completed City of Toronto mayor and city-councillor general-election contests from 2003-01-01 through 2014-12-31, plus any completed mayor/councillor by-elections in that period.  
**Approved institutional endorsers:** Progress Toronto; Toronto Star Editorial Board; Toronto Sun Editorial Board; CUPE Ontario; Elementary Teachers of Toronto (ETT); ATU Local 113.

This is a high-recall discovery report, not the final adjudication ledger. A row marked **lead** is useful for the verification queue but should not be loaded as a confirmed endorsement without checking the underlying endorser or a qualifying contemporaneous direct attribution. “No endorsement found” means no qualifying item was found in this pass; it does not prove that no endorsement occurred.

## Rules applied

An endorsement requires an explicit choice, endorsement, or formal champion selection by the exact approved entity, made public before polls closed. I excluded ratings, questionnaires, policy pledges, praise, personal/columnist choices, union or labour-council affiliates, candidate claims without corroboration, and silence.

Multiple candidates per endorser/contest are permitted. For council, the ward is part of the contest key. The announcement date is the publication date where it could be established; otherwise it is left as “not located.”

## Exclusions and false-positive controls

- A Toronto and York Region Labour Council slate is not a CUPE Ontario slate.
- A CUPE local, CUPE national, or another union affiliate is not CUPE Ontario unless the source explicitly names CUPE Ontario.
- Sue-Ann Levy’s Sun column is not the Toronto Sun Editorial Board.
- Candidate biographies and campaign claims are discovery leads only unless corroborated.
- Policy statements, transit advocacy, ratings, questionnaires, and praise are not endorsements.
- Appointed councillors do not create by-election contests.
- Ward 39’s explicit “no endorsement” in the 2010 Star slate is not an endorsement row.
- A report that an editorial board had not yet endorsed by a particular date is not evidence that it never endorsed during the remaining campaign.
