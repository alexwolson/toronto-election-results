# Toronto municipal endorsement backfill: discovery report (2015–2026)

Research date/cutoff: 2026-08-21  
Scope: City of Toronto mayor and city-councillor contests only.  
Approved endorsers: Progress Toronto; Toronto Star Editorial Board; Toronto Sun Editorial Board; CUPE Ontario; Elementary Teachers of Toronto (ETT); ATU Local 113.

This is a high-recall discovery report, not a final adjudication file. An entry is a qualifying endorsement only when the cited material explicitly endorses, selects, or tells the audience to vote for a named candidate. Pledges, ratings, questionnaires, praise, affiliations, anti-candidate campaigns, and silence are not endorsements. A candidate's own claim is retained as a lead until corroborated. Multiple candidates for one endorser/contest are allowed.

## 2026 current scan

No qualifying endorsement by any of the six exact approved entities was located for a 2026 Toronto mayor or councillor candidate by the cutoff.

Important near-misses excluded from the 2026 results:

- Progress Toronto’s [Brad Bradford pledge/campaign](https://www.progresstoronto.ca/stop-brad) is an anti-candidate campaign and explicitly does not select another candidate.
- Individual former Toronto Sun writers’ statements are not Toronto Sun Editorial Board endorsements.
