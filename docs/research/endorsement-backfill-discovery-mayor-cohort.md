# Endorsement backfill discovery: individual mayor cohort

**Research date:** 2026-08-21  
**Information cutoff:** 2026-08-20 for cohort membership; endorsement discovery
through 2026-08-21  
**Scope:** Toronto mayor and City Council contests only

This is a high-recall discovery report for the approved individual endorser cohort:

- **David Miller**, eligible from 2003-12-01;
- **John Tory**, eligible from 2014-12-01; and
- **Olivia Chow**, eligible from 2023-07-12.

The date is the first assumption of the amalgamated-Toronto mayoralty, not the
person's election date. Self-endorsement is excluded. A public statement by the
person counts when it explicitly endorses, supports, or urges a vote for a named
candidate. Praise, a campaign appearance, a congratulatory statement after voting,
and an office, staff, or party statement without the person's own position do not
count. A missing item is not a negative endorsement.

## Excluded or unresolved leads

- **John Tory's 2014 mayoral campaign:** endorsements made while Tory was a
  mayoral candidate and before 2014-12-01 are outside his cohort window. They should
  not be backfilled as former-mayor endorsements.
- **Olivia Chow's 2023 mayoral campaign:** endorsements she received before
  2023-07-12 are not endorsements made by her as a mayor. Do not reverse the edge.
- **John Tory congratulating Nick Mantas in 2021:** post-election congratulations
  are not electoral endorsements.
- **Cynthia Lai in 2022:** retain the endorsement fact if the dataset contains her
  registered candidacy; separately preserve the death-before-election / votes-not-
  counted outcome handling.
- **Campaign and office labels:** “Tory campaign,” “Team Tory,” “Chow-aligned,”
  and mayoral-office statements should not be imported as person-level endorsements
  without a source tying the statement to the named individual.
