# Luna public discovery: Toronto Star Editorial Board endorsement packages, 2003–2026

**Research date/cutoff:** 2026-08-28
**Researcher:** Luna
**Endorser:** Toronto Star Editorial Board (`toronto_star_editorial_board`)
**Scope:** Toronto mayoral and City Council contests only, including completed Toronto by-elections, from the 2003 general election through the 2026 general election. Trustees and other endorsers are out of scope.

## Result

The Star's endorsement history is best researched as election-level editorial packages. Public research identifies a package or strong bibliographic target for every general election, but the surviving public record is uneven for the older and council material. The checked-in Results data already contains the confirmed 2003 mayoral choice, the complete 2010 council slate (43 positive choices plus an explicit Ward 39 “no endorsement” statement), the 2010 mayoral choice, the 2018 and 2022 mayoral choices, the 2022 council package, and the 2023 mayoral by-election choice.

The principal unresolved public-retrieval work is the original text or page images for the 2003 council package, the 2006 mayoral and council packages, the 2014 council package, the 2018 council package, and the single-ward by-election packages. The 2026 election is still pending: as of August 28, nominations have closed, but polling is not until October 26, so no Star editorial-board endorsement package should be expected to be complete yet.

This report records positive leads and package targets only. It does not infer that the Star did not endorse a candidate when a package was not recovered.

## Public search method

I inspected the checked-in endorsement curations and prior Luna/Terra research reports, then searched public publisher indexes, search-engine results, public reproductions, local reporting, and election references using combinations of:

- `Toronto Star` / `Star editorial board` / `endorsement` / `choice`;
- the election year and election date;
- `Toronto mayor`, `Toronto city council`, ward number, and `by-election`; and
- the exact titles and publisher URLs already identified in the repository.

The City of Toronto election pages and declarations were used only to validate event, office, ward, and candidate identity. Search results, Wikipedia, campaign biographies, and later indexes were retained as retrieval leads, not imported as new facts. Direct Star pages are frequently paywalled and automated public retrieval was blocked by the publisher's robots policy during this pass; no institutional login was attempted.

## Confirmed and lead outcomes

### Existing confirmed package/fact outcomes

- **2003 mayor:** David Miller. The public reproduction identifies the Star editorial and preserves explicit choice language; this is already curated.
- **2010 mayor:** George Smitherman. A contemporaneous Toronto Life report identifies the Star's official choice; already curated.
- **2010 council:** 43 positive choices across Wards 1–38 and 40–44. The recovered reproduction explicitly says no endorsement in Ward 39; already curated without creating a negative edge.
- **2018 mayor:** John Tory. The Star-owned title is an explicit electoral choice; already curated.
- **2022 mayor:** John Tory. The Star-owned title is an explicit electoral choice; already curated.
- **2022 council:** 22 positive choices from the three-article set. Wards 2 and 21 explicitly received no choice, and Ward 16 names two candidates without selecting one; already curated under the open-world rule.
- **2023 mayoral by-election:** Ana Bailão. The Star-owned title is an explicit electoral choice; already curated.

### Holds and recovery leads

- **2003 Ward 18:** Adam Giambrone is mentioned in secondary material as a Star choice, but the original Star package or a qualifying contemporaneous direct attribution is missing.
- **2006 mayor:** David Miller has a precise print citation, “Qualified support for David Miller,” but the title alone does not preserve the editorial's choice or qualification.
- **2006 Ward 15:** Ron Singer is attributed to the Star in public election material, but the Star's Nov. 8 council editorial remains unrecovered.
- **2014 council:** public candidate pages and indexes name individual examples, but no complete package has been captured. Do not assemble a slate from those fragments.
- **2018 council:** a public reproduction recovers Wards 1–12 and other discovery material names additional wards, but the complete publisher text has not been captured. Do not import the partial list as a slate.
- **Single-ward by-elections:** no positive package was found publicly for 2016 Ward 2, 2017 Ward 42, 2021 Ward 22, 2023 Ward 20, 2024 Ward 15, or 2025 Ward 25. This is a retrieval disposition, not a no-endorsement conclusion.
