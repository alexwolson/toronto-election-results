# 2000 school trustee identity review — 2026-10-29

Scope: the 45 proposed Candidacy-Person links raised when the November 13, 2000 trustee elections for the four School Boards were added from the City's archived "Vote 2000" pages (ADR 0010). Each proposal links a 2000 trustee Candidacy, by exact display name, to a Person who already has a confirmed Candidacy. The other 73 new trustee Candidacies either matched no existing Person or were linked by the existing official incumbent-continuity rule.

Two reviewers examined all 45 blind, against the same standard as the earlier reviews: a person-specific source, or strong structural continuity (same board and office, same or overlapping ward, short gap, uncommon name, no competing same-name candidate). Exact name alone is never enough, and a link to a different office or board needs a person-specific source.

## Proposal targets corrected

Both reviewers found four packets aimed at the wrong existing Person. For Bruce Davis, Irene Atkinson, Stephnie Payne and Joseph Cohen the registry already held two People with the same name: a provincial candidate and the trustee re-elected in the same ward in 2003. The build had aimed each proposal at the earliest occurrence of the name, the provincial candidate. `extend_identity_review` now aims a name-only proposal at the one existing Person who held the same office on the same body, when several share the name (the link is still only a proposal). The four corrected packets went to two fresh blind reviewers: Davis, Atkinson and Payne were confirmed from person-specific sources (a City appointee biography and the Toronto Observer for Davis; CP24, the Globe and Mail and her obituaries for Atkinson; a Black Creek oral history and the Globe for Payne), and Cohen stays unresolved.

## Result

The reviewers agreed on 40 of the other 41. The one split, Rose Andrachuk (TCDSB Ward 2 in 2000 to the 2003 Progressive Conservative candidate in Etobicoke Centre), was confirmed by the primary reviewer: a 2011 federal appointments notice describes Rose Andrachuk of Toronto as a lawyer who was Chair and Trustee of the TCDSB, and NOW's 2003 coverage describes the candidate as a lawyer. Ann Andrachuk, first elected for TCDSB Ward 2 in 2003, is a different person and stays separate.

Result: 25 confirmed (18 person-specific, 7 structural), 20 unresolved, none rejected.

## Notes for later passes

- Probable splits across levels, without a source that joins them yet: Irene Atkinson (trustee record and the 1999 NDP Parkdale—High Park candidate), Stephnie Payne (trustee record and the 1999 NDP York West candidate, same rare spelling), Bruce Davis (trustee record and the 1995 Liberal Etobicoke—Lakeshore candidate).
- Joseph Cohen's trustee record also carries unresolved 2006 TDSB Ward 11, 2010 council Ward 10 and 2018 TDSB Ward 5 links; the name is common, so those need person-specific evidence.
- Walter Melnyk: Wikipedia's 1997 candidate note says the 1997 Ward 19 council candidate ran for the Catholic board in 2000, which joins those two runs, but neither is tied to the 2006 and 2014 Etobicoke-Lakeshore record they are compared against.
- Elizabeth Hill's Communist Party candidate biography (a York and Toronto trustee from 1988 for 18 years) may support her unresolved 2006 TDSB and 2015 federal runs.
- The 2010 TCDSB results list eight candidates in two wards on the same day (for example Jo-Ann Davis with 6,294 votes in Ward 9 and 960 in Ward 6), which suggests the 2010 poll-by-poll file assigned some polls to the wrong trustee ward. This predates the 2000 work and needs a check against the Clerk's 2010 declaration. Resolved in [the 2010 TCDSB ward assignment correction](tcdsb-2010-ward-assignment-2026-10-29.md).
- Three 2003 French-board names are stored surname-first or with a stray space ("Masson Alain", "Guerin Francois", "Leblanc- Miller").
