## Identity bridges

The Local's 2023 profile explicitly connects the 2023 Ward 20 candidate to his
2022 and 2010 council runs, and the 2022/2023 rows are a strong same-ward,
same-office continuity. However, the certified 2026 candidacy is a TDSB trustee
row, while several 2026 directory/ward sources still show a separate or stale
Ward 20 council registration and do not provide a candidate-controlled bridge to
the certified trustee candidacy. Because the office changed and the 2026 source
state is internally inconsistent, retain all three proposed occurrences as
**hold**, not confirm; the old results themselves are genuine but person linkage
to the certified 2026 trustee requires a first-party bridge.
