# Star ward previews and race stories on the identity backlog — 2026-10-30

Scope: the unresolved 1994-2006 council, mayor and trustee proposals left after
`star-profiles-backlog-2026-10-30.md`, read against the Toronto Star's ward previews, race stories,
riding stories and mayoral profiles, and against the occupations candidates gave Elections Canada.
The standard is unchanged: a person-specific source, or strong structural continuity for the same
office type with an uncommon name; a link across office types needs a person-specific source. An
occupation counts as person-specific when it matches the other run.

I found no 1997 or 2000 counterpart to the 2003 ward-by-ward preview that settled several cases in
the previous pass. The Star's 1997 coverage ran as one story per former municipality, and its 2000
coverage previewed the most contested wards. Most of what follows comes from those stories, the
2006 profile pages and the occupations candidates gave Elections Canada.

## Records joined

Assertions at the end of `PERSON_RECORD_MERGE_ASSERTIONS`:

- **Janaki Bala-Krishnan** (`janaki-bala-krishnan-1997-council-run`). The City's 1997 results print
  the Ward 11 candidate as "Janaki Bala-Krishan", a separate Person. The Star's 1997 profile prints
  her as Janaki Bala-Krishnan, a hydro engineer and community activist "who ran provincially in the
  1995 election". The 1997 record joins her 1995 and 1999 NDP record.
- **Al Chumak** (`al-chumak-1994-1997-council-runs`). The Star's 1997 story on the Toronto wards
  describes the Ward 19 candidate as "former Toronto public school board trustee Al Chumak, who ran
  a respectable second to Hutcheon in 1994", and its 1997 profile prints him as Alex Chumak. The
  1994 Ward 1 and 1997 Ward 19 records are joined.

  November 2001 the Star names Ward 12 trustee Paul Crawford as the board's vice-chair, so the 2000
  winner held the seat. The target was acclaimed in Ward 12 in 2003 and re-elected in 2006, but no
  source found calls the 2003 candidate the incumbent, and the Star's 2003 results story names a
  second incumbent trustee called Paul Crawford on the Durham public board. His 1994 Scarborough
  council run (a sales account manager) also stays open.
- Hortencia Fotopoulos: a retired restaurateur in East York in 1997 and a columnist for a
  Portuguese newspaper in Davenport in 2003. The name is very rare, but nothing ties the two runs.
- Ron Robins's 1995 and 1999 Natural Law Party runs for the legislature sit on a second Person,
  apart from his 1993 federal record that the 1994 council run now joins. Both are probably the
  same man, but the only tie is the party. The Star's coverage of the 1995 and 1999 races names
  him only by party. Wikipedia's lists of Natural Law candidates in those Ontario elections could
  not be fetched for this review, and its 1993 federal list has no entry for him.
