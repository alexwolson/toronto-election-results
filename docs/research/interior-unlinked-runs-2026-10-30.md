## Findings

**Ron McNaughton.** The Star describes him as a salesman in Scarborough Ward 12 in 1994 and as a
storekeeper in TDSB Ward 1 (Scarborough Wexford/Scarborough Agincourt) in 1997. In 2000 it lists
him as the Ward 20 (Scarborough Agincourt) incumbent, in sales. In 2006 the Ward 20 candidate,
whose record was the only one linked, is "a salesman and former trustee" seeking to return. The
1994, 1997 and 2000 runs are confirmed to that record. Nothing found covers the 2016 Ward 21
by-election (146 votes), a different ward ten years after his last run, so it stays unresolved.

**Ron Gillis.** The Star gives the same occupation, insurance broker, for Ron Gillis in each of
his five Catholic trustee races in Etobicoke: Separate School Board Ward 7 in 1994 and TCDSB Ward 2
in 1997, 2000, 2003 (where he is 50) and 2006. The 2003 and 2006 runs were already linked; 1994,
1997 and 2000 are confirmed. The Star's archive mentions other men named Ron Gillis over the
years, so the occupation does the work here and the name alone would not.

**Nellie Pedro.** Her only linked record was the 2003 Liberal candidacy in Trinity-Spadina, and
the Star's 2003 riding profile calls that candidate a school trustee and CHIN television
personality. She won TDSB Ward 9 (Davenport) in 2000, when the Star described her as the host of
her own TV show, and the 2006 Ward 9 profile says she was trustee from 2000 to 2003. Both trustee
runs are confirmed. Her 1994 Toronto Board of Education race covered city wards 11 and 12 (The
Junction, and Davenport and Corso Italia), which overlap the 2000 Ward 9 seat. It is confirmed on
Movimento Perpétuo's archive of Portuguese-Canadian candidates, which lists her 1994 trustee run
and her 2003 Liberal run, together with the successor-board continuity and the rare name. The
Star's 1994 profile describes her as a real estate agent at the time.

**Joseph Martino.** The 2003 Ward 1 winner "Joe Martino" was held as his own Person. The Star's
2003 profile says he was trustee for 15 years before the last election, and its 2006 profile of
the re-elected Ward 1 trustee says 18 years; that service is the 1994 Separate School Board and
1997 TCDSB record. The 2003 record now redirects to the main one
(`joseph-martino-2003-return-to-tcdsb`). In 2000 he ran for council in Ward 2 (598 votes) instead
of seeking re-election, and the Star's council profile calls him a five-term Catholic school
trustee, so that run is confirmed too. The pending 2026 council proposal is untouched.

**Doug Stephens.** The 1994 North York Ward 13 winner was profiled in 1997 as a sitting trustee
in TDSB Ward 13 (Seneca Heights), which he won. In 2000 the Star lists him as the Ward 17 (Don
Valley East) incumbent seeking a third term, and Shelley Carroll's
[Wikipedia biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelley_Carroll) says she unseated the
incumbent there and succeeded him. These three runs had no Person, and they become one new Person
(`doug-stephens-1994-2000-north-york-trustee`). They are not joined to the 2006 Ward 12
(Willowdale) candidate, the only Doug Stephens record before this change. The Star's 2006 profile
called that candidate the incumbent, but its correction the next day says Mari Rutka was the
incumbent, and no source names him as the former trustee. The archive also has other Doug
Stephenses (a Courtice letter writer, a retail consultant).
