# Incumbent Toronto councillor linkage audit — 2026-08-21

## Scope and sources

The official [City of Toronto Members of Council roster](https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/council/members-of-council/) states that Council has 25 ward councillors and lists the current members for Wards 1–25. I treated that roster as the 2026-08-20 cutoff roster (the page was checked 2026-08-21), and added Mayor Olivia Chow. Chris Alexander, the 2026 mayoral candidate, is explicitly out of scope here; his separate report handles that linkage. The City’s [councillor history](https://www.toronto.ca/explore-enjoy/history-art-culture/mayors-councillors-reeves-chairmen/city-of-toronto-councillors/) and [official election-results index](https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/elections/election-results-reports/) were used as corroborating primary sources.

I compared those people with `data/out/election_results.csv`, `data/out/people.csv`, `data/out/candidacy_person_links.csv`, and `data/reference/identity_review_dispositions.csv`. “Linked” below means the release row has the canonical active Person ID. A `NULL` is a real official result occurrence with no Person ID. The link ledger also retains closed historical confirmed rows for deprecated duplicate IDs; those rows have a release end date and redirect to the canonical active IDs in `people.csv`, so they are not evidence of a second person or a same-event collision.

## Result

There are **126** in-scope candidacies: **126 linked** and **0 null**. No same-event duplicate candidate-name collision was found for the roster people. No same-office incumbency anomaly was found in the release: the `incumbent` values follow the project rule that incumbency is body + office type, not merely the person’s presence elsewhere. In particular, a councillor running for mayor, an MPP/trustee moving to Council, a first-time winner, or a by-election winner is correctly not marked incumbent.
