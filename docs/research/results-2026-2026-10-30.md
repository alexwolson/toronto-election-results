# 2026 results and the 2026 identity proposals — 2026-10-30

## Results

The City Clerk certified the 2026 municipal election on 29 October 2026. The Declaration of Results
([PDF](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/8966-Declaration-of-Results-2026-Toronto-Municipal-Election-Oct-29-Final.pdf))
is pinned at `data/reference/results_2026/declaration_of_results_2026.pdf` and transcribed to
`data/reference/results_2026_declaration.csv` by `scripts/transcribe_2026_declaration.py`. It
gives a vote total for each of the 357 contested Candidacies and marks the four acclaimed trustees.
Every printed name matches the City's certified candidate list exactly, so the loader applies the
results one to one and rejects any mismatch, tie for first place or disagreement between the
printed winner and the vote totals.

The declaration prints no "X" beside the winner of Conseil scolaire Viamonde Ward 3. Its top
vote-getter, Anna-Karyna Ruszkowski (1,806 votes to 846), is set in bold like every other winner,
and the declaration elects "the candidate who received the highest number of votes for each
office". The loader names this one Contest as a known omission and derives its winner from the
votes.

The declaration gives no electorate or ballots cast, so `eligible_electors`, `ballots_cast` and
`turnout` stay null for 2026 until the City publishes its final voter statistics.

## Follow-up: incumbency and runs the guides name

`incumbent` for the 2026 council and mayoral races was null because the council roster that the
incumbency rule reads stopped at the 2018-2022 term. The City's 2022-2026 attendance and voting
exports (`members-of-toronto-city-council-meeting-attendance`, refreshed 15 August 2026) now
supply it: 26 members, the 25 councillors and Mayor Olivia Chow, including the by-election winners
Parthi Kandavel, Rachel Chernos Lin and Neethan Shan. The 2022-2026 attendance export renames its
columns ("First Name", "Session Date"), which the reader now accepts. 22 sitting councillors and
the mayor ran in 2026; Brad Bradford, a councillor running for mayor, is not a mayoral incumbent.
Candidacies without a Person keep a null `incumbent`.

The guides also name earlier runs that were unlinked. Reviewer
`unlinked-runs-2026-guides-2026-10-30` confirms nine rows:

- Nadia Guerrera's 2022 Liberal run in York South—Weston (Wikipedia's 2026 election page;
  TorontoToday calls her 2025 run her third as a Liberal).
- Mary Fragedakis's 2022 Liberal run in Toronto—Danforth (The Local's 2026 Ward 14 guide).
- Cleveland Marshall's 2023 mayoral run (33rd of 102) and 2025 independent run in Toronto Centre
  (The Local's 2026 Ward 13 guide). His 2024 Ward 15 by-election run stays unresolved; no source
  names it.
- John Kladitis's 2017 Ward 42 by-election, 2018 and 2022 provincial and 2019 federal runs, and his
  2026 Ward 14 run. The 2017 run follows his 2014 Ward 41 run in the neighbouring Scarborough ward
  under a very rare name; The Local ties the 2026 candidate to 2017 and 2019, and his campaign
  site says he has run at all three levels.

Two curated assertions:

- `han-dong-2026-council-run` joins Han Dong's 2026 Ward 23 run to his Person. The Local's guide
  describes him as the former Trinity-Spadina MPP and Don Valley North MP.
- `bob-murphy-ward-12-runs-2018-2026` makes one new Person of Bob Murphy's 2018, 2022 and 2026
  Ward 12 runs and 2023 mayoral run. The Local's 2026 and 2022 guides tie the four together. The
  2014 Ward 22 candidate, in the same part of the city, keeps his own record: the name is common
  and no source names that run.
