# Toronto Star endorsement packages recovered through U of T Libraries

**Retrieval date:** 2026-08-28
**Access route:** University of Toronto Libraries institutional access to ProQuest, Toronto Star publication record 44892
**Scope:** Toronto mayor and council endorsement packages needed for the 2003–2026 endorsement census
**Handling rule:** This file records bibliographic metadata and concise factual summaries only. It does not reproduce licensed article text or retain credentials, session URLs, or downloaded copies.

## Evidentiary use

Each ProQuest record below was opened in full text through the authenticated U of T Libraries session. The editorial language was checked ward by ward. A candidate appears in the positive-choice list only where the article made a single electoral choice or endorsement. Acclamations, praise without a choice, and explicit refusals to endorse are retained as package observations rather than positive endorsement edges.

Stable locators use the ProQuest document identifier without transient search-session parameters.
