# Star candidate profiles on the identity backlog — 2026-10-30

Scope: unresolved council, mayor and trustee proposals from the 1994, 1997, 2000, 2003 and 2006
municipal elections, checked against the Toronto Star's printed candidate profiles. The interior
runs for McNaughton, Stephens, Gillis, Pedro and Martino were settled first
(`interior-unlinked-runs-2026-10-30.md`); this pass applies the same pages to the rest of the
backlog. A follow-up then checked the weakest confirmations and the runs the profiles had left
apart against the Star's race stories, ward previews, riding stories and columns. The standard is
unchanged: a person-specific source, or strong structural continuity for the same office type with
an uncommon name; a link across office types needs a person-specific source. A Star profile counts
as person-specific when it names a past office ("a former school trustee", "a York councillor") or
gives an occupation or platform that matches the other run.

## Records joined

Assertions at the end of `PERSON_RECORD_MERGE_ASSERTIONS`:

- **Worrick Russell** (`worrick-russell-scarborough-council-runs`). The City's 1997 results list
  the Ward 15 candidate as "Russell Worrick", a separate Person. The Star prints him as Worrick
  Russell, a paralegal and mediator, and calls the 2003 Ward 35 candidate "the mediator". The
  1997 record joins the 2000-2006 Ward 35 record, with the 1994 Metro race in Scarborough City
  Centre. The 1997 candidacy keeps the name as the City printed it.
- **André Duclos** (`andre-duclos-monavenir-2000-2003`). The 2000 Ward 3 incumbent and the 2003
  Ward 4 candidate, whom the Star's 2003 profile says looks forward to serving his constituents "as
  he has in the past". This reverses the decision in `variant-name-splits-2026-10-29.md` to keep
  them apart. His 1994 French-section win stays unresolved: the Star gives a different occupation
  and the areas do not clearly overlap.
- **Nicolò Fortunato** (`nicolo-fortunato-former-york-councillor`). The 1997 Ward 5 candidate,
  "a former York councillor", joins the 2000 Ward 2 record ("a three-term York councillor"). It had
  been a claim on the 2003 "Nicolo Fortunato" record, which the Star does not describe.
- **Mike Thomas** (`mike-thomas-1994-scarborough-council-1995-pc`). The Star's 1995 Scarborough
  North riding story says the Progressive Conservative candidate, a 21-year-old York University
  political science student, finished second to Bas Balkissoon in the previous November's
  municipal election. That is Scarborough Ward 13 in 1994, where the Star's profile calls Michael
  Thomas a student and its story on young candidates calls him a 21-year-old York political science
  student. The 1994 council record joins the 1995 provincial record.

Found in passing in the 2003 ward-by-ward preview:

- **Adrian Heaps** (`adrian-heaps-1999-liberal-candidate`). The preview calls the Ward 35
  candidate "a former provincial Liberal candidate", and the Star's 1999 Scarborough Southwest
  riding story names him as the Liberal candidate there. His Wikipedia biography covers both. The
  1999 record joins the 2003-2010 council record.
- **Paulette Senior** (`paulette-senior-2003-council-run`). The preview says the Ward 42 candidate,
  an NDP activist, "ran federally in 2000 and provincially in 1999". The 2003 council record joins
  the record of those two NDP runs in Scarborough—Rouge River.

confirmed to the 1999 record, and the two trustee proposals to that record are rejected.
`school-boards-1994-1997-2026-10-29.md` had read the two 1994 rows as one man running for both
offices; both rows stand, but they are two people.

## Left as they were

Runs the sources leave open keep their rows unresolved. Among them:

- Stephen Thiele's 1994 Toronto Board of Education race. The Star calls the candidate a lawyer, as
  the later sources do, but the race was in the old City of Toronto and the later runs are in
  Etobicoke, so the link rests on the name and occupation alone.
- Bert Dandy's 1997 TCDSB Ward 12 race. The 2000 TDSB candidate is the lawyer who sat on the
  Scarborough Board of Education as one of its separate school representatives until 1986 (Star,
  1982 and 1986). That fits a Catholic board run, but the Star's 1997 profile calls the TCDSB
  candidate a restaurateur and no source puts the lawyer in that race.
- Mike Thomas's trustee runs against the 1994-1995 record. The trustee was also described as a
  student in 1997 and ran against Balkissoon in 2000, but no source says he is the PC candidate,
  and the name is common.
- Bruce Brackett, the 2003 Ward 30 candidate, was 64 and the owner and publisher of a community
  newspaper. Terry Brackett was 51 in 1994, so they are two people, and his run keeps its own
  record.
- Cross-office runs where only the name and area match: Ann Andrachuk, Larry Faseruk, Paul Raina,
  Stuart Weinstein, Tony Marzilli, Caryl Manning, Bob Allisat, Michael Green, Margo Duncan,
  Martha Marini and the federal and provincial candidates (Settatree, Calandra,
  Luciani, Sasso, Figliano, Hum, Perkins, Mortazavi, Browne, Senater, Moore, McGirr).
- Same-office runs with a common name and no matching detail: Oliver Carroll, Paul Crawford, Errol
  Young, John Campbell, Fred Dominelli, Peter Kell, Jim McMillan, Ron Singer, Suzanne McCormick,
  Joseph Cohen, Michael Sullivan.
- John Steele (1994 and 2000 mayor) is a Communist League member in the 1994 profile, but his 2003
  provincial run is listed as independent, so the party does not carry the link.
