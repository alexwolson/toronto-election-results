# The 2018–2022 municipal backlog (2026-10-31)

This pass took the 98 municipal candidacies from 2018 to 2022 that were still unresolved after the passes in `local-guides-backlog-2026-10-30.md`, and searched each one in the Toronto Star on ProQuest (University of Toronto access), including pages ProQuest holds only as scans. The Star printed most 2022 runs and most school trustee runs only in candidate lists and results tables, so the 2022 rows were also checked against the trustee sections of The Local's 2022 ward guides. A few rows were settled from other pages found along the way. The standard is the one used throughout: the source has to name the other run, or describe the person with a distinctive fact that matches the other run. Dispositions are under reviewer `backlog-2018-2022-2026-10-31`.

Twenty-seven of the 98 rows are confirmed to their targets, and the same sources confirm seven runs outside the backlog. Seventy-one rows remain unresolved. Each Star quote was re-read in ProQuest and each guide entry on The Local's own page.

## Left unresolved

For most of the 71 open rows the Star prints the unlinked run only in a candidate list or results table, or not at all. Twenty-five rows are weak matches: the coverage names the candidate in the same area, or describes one of the runs, without a fact shared with the other. The closest are:

- Dean Maher, 2018 Ward 10: the 2010 Ward 20 candidate proposed banning the sale of dogs and cats in pet stores. The 2018 candidate founded the CityPlace Residents' Association, and a 2012 story says he had lived in CityPlace, then inside Ward 20, since 2003. No story ties the two runs.
- Sonny Cho, 2018 Ward 18: the Star endorsed the 2010 candidate as a commercial real estate broker with Liberal roots, and in 2018 it mentions his 2014 bid for the federal Liberal nomination in Willowdale.
- Joyce Rowlands, 2018 Ward 11: the 2007 Liberal candidate was June Rowlands's daughter and a former occupational health nurse. The 2018 coverage gives no description.
- George Sawision, 2018 Ward 11: environmental and cycling activism in 2007 to 2010 coverage, and a cycling platform in 2018.
- Catherina Perez, Amanda Cain, Taras Kulish, Edward Zaretsky, Troy Young, Paulina Corpuz, Fawzi Bidawi, Peter D'Gama, Paul Maguire and others: the descriptions are consistent but generic, or only one of the two runs is described.
- Bonnie Hu, 2022 Ward 3: The Local's guide names a federal Libertarian run in South Surrey–White Rock, which is not the target's 2018 Ontario Libertarian run in York South–Weston.

Other notes:

- Chris Jones, 2022 TDSB Ward 16: The Local describes a father of two with a background in business and marketing who chairs a Beach daycare board. The Star described the target, the 2014 NDP candidate in Etobicoke Centre, as a senior planner with the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority. Nothing ties them.
- George Stevens, 2018 TDSB Ward 9: The Local's 2022 entry names his 2014 Davenport council run, which the target already holds with the 2022 run, but not the 2018 run.
- The Local has no information on Robb Cooke, Markpaul St.Bishoy or David Moore, and Opa Hope Day's entry names no earlier run.
- Mark Balack, 2018 Ward 9: as described in the 2010–2017 note.

None of the page images opened in this pass bears on an open row. Most were death notices, advertisements, listings or results tables.
