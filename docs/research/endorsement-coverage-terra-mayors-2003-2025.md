# Terra endorsement coverage: individual Toronto mayors (2003–2025)

**Research date:** 2026-08-22  
**Scope:** public, pre-poll personal endorsements by David Miller (eligible from
2003-12-01), John Tory (from 2014-12-01), and Olivia Chow (from 2023-07-12),
of a Toronto Mayor or City Councillor candidate, through the end of 2025.

This is an open-world coverage note. It records only affirmative evidence and
the limits of a search; it does not make candidate-level negative assertions.
The existing mayor-cohort discovery and verification reports were read first.
Their already curated facts are excluded from the new-positive table below.

## Rule and target validation

An item clears this pass only where a named individual is explicitly reported as
endorsing/supporting a named in-scope candidate before the poll. Praise,
appearances, shared policy, opposition to another candidate, and after-result
congratulations do not clear that gate. Candidate keys were checked against the
City-Clerk-derived `data/out/election_results.csv`; the City also provides the
[2018 declaration](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/97da-2018clerksofficialdeclarationofresults.pdf),
[2022 declaration](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/9085-FinalDeclaration-of-Results-for-the-2022-Toronto-Municipal-Election.pdf),
[2021 Ward 22 declaration](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/8e1c-Results_Official_Declaration_2021_By-Election_Ward-22.pdf),
[2023 mayoral declaration](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/8eef-Declaration-of-Results-for-the-2023-Toronto-By-Election-for-Mayor-Final.pdf),
and [2025 Ward 25 declaration](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/97f1-Declaration-of-Results-Councillor-Ward-25-ByElection.pdf).

The existing curation already contains 16 mapped individual-mayor facts:
Miller → Pantalone (2010 mayor); Tory → Grimes, Mihevc and Bradford (2018);
Tory's 11 mapped 2022 council targets; and Tory → Bailão (2023 mayor). They
were deliberately not counted again. The separately well-supported 2022 Tory →
Cynthia Lai item remains a mapping hold because the released result file has no
Lai Candidacy.

## Retrieval notes and disposition counts

The principal 2006 community-newspaper URLs were discovered with their dated
verbatim index excerpts. Their pages could not be re-opened through the final
retrieval cache, so the links and quoted attribution should be retained for
future archival capture; this is a source-access limitation, not a reason to
silently erase the direct contemporaneous attribution. The accessible Spacing
source independently confirms the 2010 fact.

- **New confirmed positive assertions:** 3 (all Miller)
- **Existing confirmed facts intentionally excluded:** 16 mapped assertions;
  plus one valid-but-unmapped Tory → Lai historical hold
- **New holds:** 0
- **Non-qualifying/rejected leads documented:** 4 (Tory → Mantas 2021,
  Tory/Chernos-Lin 2024 strategy, Miller → Chow 2023 congratulations, Chow →
  Chernos Lin 2024 congratulations)
- **Coverage batches searched:** 19; 17 remain incomplete/open-world, one has
  existing comprehensive source coverage (Tory 2022 council), and one contains
  verified positives but an incomplete slate (Miller 2006 council).

No CSV, reference curation, or generated output was changed by this research
pass. The three `CONFIRM` rows are candidates for a subsequent curation review,
not an automatic data import.
