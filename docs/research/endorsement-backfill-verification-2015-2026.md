# Toronto municipal endorsement backfill: verification audit (2015–2026)

Research date/cutoff: 2026-08-21  
Audited input: [discovery report](endorsement-backfill-discovery-2015-2026.md)  
Scope: the approved institutional panel only — Progress Toronto; Toronto Star Editorial
Board; Toronto Sun Editorial Board; CUPE Ontario; Elementary Teachers of Toronto (ETT);
and ATU Local 113. Targets are City of Toronto Mayor and City Councillor Candidacies only.

## Method and result-record check

`CONFIRM` requires an exact approved Endorser, an explicit public electoral choice/support
made before polls closed, and a named candidate who appears in the City's official record.
`HOLD` is a plausible lead that does not yet clear that source gate. `REJECT` is material
that is not an endorsement under the project definition. No result below creates a negative
edge for other candidates.

The City's [2018 declaration](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/97da-2018clerksofficialdeclarationofresults.pdf),
[2022 declaration](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/9085-FinalDeclaration-of-Results-for-the-2022-Toronto-Municipal-Election.pdf),
[2023 mayoral declaration](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/8eef-Declaration-of-Results-for-the-2023-Toronto-By-Election-for-Mayor-Final.pdf),
[2021 Ward 22 declaration](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/8e1c-Results_Official_Declaration_2021_By-Election_Ward-22.pdf),
[2023 Ward 20 certified-candidate page](https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/elections/ward-20-scarborough-southwest-by-election-list-of-candidates-third-party-advertisers/),
and [2025 Ward 25 declaration](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/97f1-Declaration-of-Results-Councillor-Ward-25-ByElection.pdf)
validate every named completed-election target below. For 2026, the official City candidate
list/JSON is the appropriate candidate record, but this audit found no qualifying edge.

## Coverage-state audit

The project definition makes coverage one **Endorser × Contest** state, whereas the discovery
matrix records one state for a whole election event. That aggregation is not import-ready: a
general election contains the Mayor contest and 25 separate Council contests. The matrix should
therefore be split before data loading. This is a data-granularity correction, not a claim that
an endorser did or did not support anyone else.

| Event / endorser | Audit finding | Recommended handling |
|---|---|---|
| 2016 Ward 2 and 2017 Ward 42, all six | No source-complete slate or auditable negative was found in this review. | Keep `partially_searched`; do not turn it into a negative. |
| Progress Toronto before 2018 | Its own history places the public champion process from 2018 onward. | `not_applicable` is defensible for earlier events; 2018 onward remains event/contest-specific. |
| 2018 Star | The Mayor contest has an explicit Tory choice; council-editorial bodies remain inaccessible. | Split to Mayor `comprehensive_source_found`; every Council contest `source_unavailable` (or `partially_searched` only after separate current searches). |
| 2022 Star | The Mayor contest has an explicit Tory choice; the three council-editorial pages remain unrecovered. | Split to Mayor `comprehensive_source_found`; Council contests `source_unavailable`. |
| 2018 Sun | Tory's mayoral choice is confirmed, but no complete council source is available. | Split to Mayor `comprehensive_source_found`; Council contests retain `partially_searched`. |
| 2023 mayoral by-election | Progress Toronto, Star, CUPE Ontario, ETT, and ATU Local 113 each have a direct explicit choice. Sun's item remains a HOLD. | The five confirmed entities may be `comprehensive_source_found` under the project's definition (“a source that presents a slate or explicit choice was located”); Sun should be `partially_searched`, not comprehensive. |
