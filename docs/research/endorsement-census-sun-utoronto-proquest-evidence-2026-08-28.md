- **Ward 15:** The section heading names “Jon Burnside or Jaye Robinson.” The explanation says both incumbents are quality candidates, that the board struggled to choose one, and that voters would be well served by either. This is a genuine two-candidate positive outcome, not an unresolved extraction error.
