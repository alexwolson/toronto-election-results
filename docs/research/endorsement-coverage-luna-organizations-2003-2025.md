# Historical endorsement coverage: ETT, ATU Local 113, Progress Toronto, and CUPE Ontario

**Research date:** 2026-08-22  
**Scope:** completed Toronto municipal mayor and city-councillor contests, 2003–2025.  
**Exact endorsers:** Elementary Teachers of Toronto (ETT); Amalgamated Transit Union Local 113; Progress Toronto; and CUPE Ontario.

This is a source-coverage report, not a negative-endorsement ledger. “Searched-no-positive-found” means that the named search batch did not locate a qualifying positive source; it does **not** mean that the endorser did not endorse anyone. “Partial positive leads” records a named candidate or electoral-support signal that still needs the project’s final adjudication gate. “Unavailable archive” records an exact-entity archive path that could not be retrieved or was not date-indexed well enough to establish a complete source set.

## Rules and event universe

I required an explicit pre-poll electoral choice or electoral support by the exact approved entity. I did not transfer support between CUPE Ontario, CUPE locals, CUPE sector bodies, ATU Canada, ATU Local 113, ETFO/other teacher locals, Toronto and York Region Labour Council, coalitions, or candidate claims. Issue advocacy, anti-candidate campaigns, questionnaires, ratings, praise, post-election congratulations, and policy statements are not positive endorsement facts.

The official City election-results index supplies the 2003, 2006, 2010, and 2014 general elections and the later by-elections: [general-election results](https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/elections/election-results-reports/election-results/general-election-results/) and [by-election results](https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/elections/election-results-reports/election-results/by-election-results/). ## Cells deliberately excluded

Following the instruction to skip cells already confirmed or marked comprehensive in the current outputs, I did not re-search these source sets: CUPE Ontario—2010 mayor and 2023 mayor; ETT—2014 mayor, 2018 mayor, 2021 Ward 22, and 2023 mayor; Progress Toronto—2022 council slate, 2023 mayor, and 2025 Ward 25; and ATU Local 113—2022 council slate, 2023 mayor, and 2023 Ward 20. The existing reports contain the evidence and citations for those rows. The current output also marks Progress Toronto—2024 Ward 15 comprehensive; it is therefore excluded here even though the existing verification report correctly warns that the anti-Anthony-Furey campaign is not an endorsement of Rachel Chernos Lin.

Progress Toronto’s own 2023 explanation says its public champion process operated “in every election since 2018” ([process statement](https://www.progresstoronto.ca/media-release-may10-progressive-mayoral-champions)). I therefore treated Progress Toronto’s 2003–2017 cells as organization-process `not_applicable`, rather than manufacturing historical negative observations.

- **2 partial-positive source batches** produced direct first-party Progress Toronto leads: 2018 Ward 3 (Amber Morley) and 2021 Ward 22 (Manna Wong).
- **1 partial-positive source batch** produced an exact-entity unresolved CUPE lead: 2003 mayor (David Miller; “CUPE” is not yet proven to mean CUPE Ontario).
- **10 organization-process `not_applicable` batches** are Progress Toronto’s pre-2018 cells; they are not searched negative facts.
- Of the remaining 52 batches, **39 were searched-no-positive-found** and **10 were unavailable archive**. No negative candidate facts were created.

Recommended next adjudication: recover an archived timestamp/capture for the Progress Toronto Manna Wong page; preserve the Progress Toronto 2018 page as first-party support evidence; and retrieve the March 5, 2003 National Post item (or a contemporaneous direct CUPE Ontario record) before considering CUPE Ontario → David Miller.
