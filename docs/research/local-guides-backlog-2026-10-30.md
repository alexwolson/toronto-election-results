# The Local's ward guides against the 2018–2026 municipal backlog (2026-10-30)

This pass took the 210 unresolved municipal candidacies from 2018 to 2026 and searched for a source that names each candidate's earlier run. The main sources were The Local's 2022 ward guides and its 2023, 2024 and 2025 by-election guides, which list a candidate's prior runs with placings or vote totals. Wikipedia's 2022 and 2023 mayoral election pages and a few other pages filled gaps.

Forty-seven dispositions were revised from `unresolved` to `confirmed` under reviewer `local-guides-backlog-2026-10-30`. Each rationale names the earlier run the source describes and the placing or vote total that matches the target's record. Where a guide gave only a vote total (Michelle Garcia, Dimitre Popov, Daryl Christoff, Knia Singh), the total was checked against `election_results.csv` before the row was confirmed. 

Three rows the guides supported (Sandeep Srivastava 2022 mayor, Sonny Yeung 2022 trustee, Gregory Rodriguez 2022 trustee) were left as they were, because curated assertions already link them to the target. The same applies to the Habiba Desai, Peter Handjis, Kevin Clarke and Dennis Hastings rows that appeared in the backlog.

## Left unresolved

These had a source, but it did not meet the evidence standard:

- Serge Korovitsyn 2023: the source gives only his Ontario Libertarian Party role.
- Lekan Olawoye 2018: the link to 2014 comes from an article's context, and no sentence states it.
- Deanna Sgro 2018: the source matches only the riding and year of a provincial run.
- Kiri Vadivelu 2023: the source covers only the 2022 run.
- Peter De Marco 2026 and Thomas Hall 2023: The Local says only "same name".
- Walter Alvarez-Bardales 2025: the two guides describe Liberal runs in different ridings.
- Aaron Anderson 2025: the guide says he "seems to have" run in 2022.

Bob Murphy's 2026 Ward 12 guide entry describes the Ward 12 Bob Murphy already grouped in an earlier pass, so it gives no support to the 2014 Ward 22 pairing. Virginia Jones's 2017 Ward 42 run does not match the 2003 Ward 38 candidacy she was paired with.

## Further runs named by the guides

The same guides name earlier runs that were also unlinked, and five more dispositions were confirmed from them:

- Edith Pearson's 2018 TCDSB Ward 6 run (second of three).
- Anna Sidiropoulos's 2022 TDSB Ward 18 run (second of eight).
- Rosina Bonavota's 2010, 2014 and 2022 runs. The Local's 2022 Ward 10 guide lists her 2014 Ward 21 run (fourth of four), her 2010 Ward 5 trustee run (third) and a 2006 run in third of seven. It calls the 2006 run a Ward 18 trustee race, but the records show no such candidacy, and her 2006 Ward 15 council run also placed third of seven, so the office is taken to be the guide's error. Her 2026 TCDSB Ward 9 run stays on hold under the trustee-career review, which needs a source that names the 2026 filer.

Senai Iman's 2018 and 2022 TDSB runs become one new Person (`senai-iman-tdsb-runs-2018-2022`). The Local's 2022 guide says the 2022 candidate placed last in Ward 19 in 2018 with 500 votes, which matches the 2018 record. Neither run is joined to the 2006 TCDSB candidate.

With these, candidacies without a Person fell from 818 to 764.

About 120 rows still have no source. Most are 2018 trustee and council candidacies, which no guide by The Local covers unless the candidate ran again later.
