# Pre-2004 federal identity review — 2026-10-29

Scope: the 78 proposed Candidacy-Person links raised when the 1993, 1997 and 2000 federal general elections and the 1999 York West by-election were added (ADR 0010). Each packet went to two independent reviewers working blind against the same standard as the provincial review: a person-specific source, or strong structural continuity (same office, same or successor district, same party, short gap, uncommon name, no competing same-name candidate). Exact name alone is never enough, and a cross-office link needs a person-specific source. Elections Canada's 1997 and 2000 occupation and residence listings count as supporting evidence.

The reviewers agreed on 74 of 78. Of the four splits, the primary reviewer confirmed three after reading the cited pages and checking their vote totals against the official results (David Gordon, Roger Carter, Steve Rutchinski), and left Dan King (1997 Don Valley West) unresolved because the evidence is structural only and the Dan King records already look like more than one person.

Result: 41 confirmed, 37 unresolved, none rejected.

## Follow-up merges and the 1996 by-election

- Roger Carter's 1993 and 2000 Person record is merged into his 2004–2015 Beaches—East York record (assertion `roger-carter-1993-2000-house-continuity`), on the CPC-ML 2006 candidate biography that gives his runs from 1993 to 2006.
- Steve Rutchinski's 1993, 1997 (as Stephen Rutchinski) and 2000 records are merged (assertion `steve-rutchinski-1993-2000-house-continuity`) on the same biography.
- The 1996 Etobicoke North by-election was added from Elections Canada's 1996 by-election synopsis. Six of its seven candidates linked automatically; Joe Peschisolido's proposed link to his 1993 Etobicoke North record was confirmed by the primary reviewer on his Wikipedia biography, which names both runs.
