# Newspaper editorial-board endorsement archive recovery

**Research date:** 2026-08-21  
**Scope:** Toronto Star Editorial Board and Toronto Sun Editorial Board endorsements in the approved Toronto mayoral and councillor dataset. The requested targets are Star 2003 (mayor and Ward 18), Star 2006 (mayor and Ward 15), the full Star council slates in 2010 and 2014, Star mayor/council choices in 2018 and 2022, and the Sun 2018 council slate.

## Evidence rule

An endorsement is importable only when the exact institutional entity is identified and the source gives an explicit positive choice, with a publication date before polls closed. A publisher page, preserved publisher page, library/newspaper reproduction, or reputable contemporaneous direct attribution is preferred. Search snippets, Wikipedia, campaign biographies, and later forum indexes are discovery aids only unless the underlying attributed content is recoverable.

An omitted candidate is not a negative endorsement. “No endorsement” is recorded only where the board explicitly says so. Columnist recommendations are not editorial-board recommendations. City Clerk results below validate the contest, ward, and canonical candidate name; they do not prove an endorsement.

## Source and identity cautions

- Use the exact institutional entity **Toronto Star Editorial Board** or **Toronto Sun Editorial Board** in the endorsement record. “Toronto Star” or “Toronto Sun” in a candidate biography is not enough unless it explicitly attributes the editorial-board choice.
- Validate names and wards against the applicable [City Clerk declarations](https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/elections/election-results-reports/). In particular, use **Richard Gosling** for 2010 Ward 12, **Ana Bailão** where the official record uses the diacritic, and do not create an alias candidate from the reproduction's “Rick Gosling” or ASCII “Ana Bailao.”
- Do not turn the Star's explicit 2010 Ward 39 “No endorsement here” into a negative endorsement edge. It is a distinct, observed board statement.
- Do not treat an absent ward in a partial or inaccessible article as a non-endorsement. The open-world endorsement rule applies throughout this recovery.
