# By-elections from 1995 to 2011 (2026-10-31)

This pass checked whether every by-election inside the coverage window is in the release. The
federal and provincial by-elections were already complete when compared with Wikipedia's lists of
by-elections for each Parliament and legislature. Five municipal by-elections were missing. They
are now in `data/reference/municipal_by_elections_1995_2002.csv`, and this note records where each
came from and how the identity proposals they raised were settled.

## The City's index of past results

The City's archived "Previous elections results" page
([Wayback capture of 4 January 2012](https://web.archive.org/web/20120104024728/http://www.toronto.ca/elections/results/index.htm))
lists every election the City ran from 1998. Besides the general elections it lists four
by-elections: East York (Ward 1) in 1998, Ward 16 in 1999, Ward 31 in 2001 and Toronto District
School Board Ward 1 in 2002. It lists none from 2003 to 2011, for council or for any school board.
Event enumeration is therefore complete from 1998, and the build manifest no longer marks trustee
by-elections before 2012 as uncertain.

The six former municipalities left no comparable index. The manifest keeps municipal by-elections
from 1995 to 1997 as `uncertain`.

## North York Ward 1, 1995

The City's archive has no page for this by-election, and no North York return was found. The
Star's report of 22 September 1995 gives the winner's total (George Mammoliti, 2,606) and the
last-place total (Vincenzo Scida, 65), and names the other four in finishing order: Tenio
Evangelista, "less than 400 votes behind", then Claudio Polsinelli, Don Parravano and Vince
Corriero. The Contest is `partial`. The four missing totals stay null, and so do the Contest's
total votes, vote shares and ranks. The Star also gives 26,888 eligible voters and a turnout just
over 25 per cent; as for every municipal Contest before 2003, the release leaves electorate and
turnout null. No complete count was found in the Star or in the other newspapers on ProQuest.

## Searching the Star for 1995 to 1997

To find any other by-election the former municipalities held between the 1994 and 1997 general
elections, I searched the Toronto Star on ProQuest from 15 November 1994 to 9 November 1997 for
by-election stories about the six councils, Metro council and the school boards, about 180
results in all. The search found the North York Ward 1 by-election and no other. Two vacancies
were settled without a vote:

- John Hastings left Etobicoke council when he won Etobicoke-Rexdale in June 1995, and the council
  appointed the runner-up from the 1994 vote to spare the cost of a by-election
  ([Star editorial, 21 September 1995](https://www.proquest.com/docview/437326715)).
- Mario Silva won the 1994 race in the old City of Toronto's Ward 3 over Tony O'Donohue, 2,976 to
  2,961. Two recounts then put O'Donohue ahead, a second judicial recount declared Silva the
  winner by 10 votes, and the Court of Appeal upheld that result on 14 December 1995
  ([Star, 15 December 1995](https://www.proquest.com/docview/437400153)). There was no new vote.
  No source found gives the totals of the final recount, so the release keeps the City's figures,
  which are the election-night count.

### Other changes

The City's archive prints the 1994 East York Ward 1 winner as "Tzirstas, Michael", and that run
was its own Person. The Star's preview of 11 August 1998 says "Michael Tziretas, who served on the
former East York council from 1994 to 1997, came fourth in the last election", and its Ward 31
report of 2001 says he "was a councillor on the former East York council from 1994 to 1997". The
1994 record now joins the Tziretas record (`michael-tziretas-1994-east-york-council-run`). The
same August preview shows that Tziretas first entered the 1998 East York race. He is absent from
the Globe and Mail's list of 3 September and from the City's results, so he did not stay on the
ballot.

The 1998 East York candidate printed as Dide Maxted keeps her own record. The name may be a short
form of Diana-De, but no source connects the two.
