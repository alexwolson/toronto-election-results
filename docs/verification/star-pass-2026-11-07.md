# Search of the Toronto Star for undetermined name pairs, November 2026

The same-name and near-name reviews ([`same-name-review-2026-11-03.md`](same-name-review-2026-11-03.md),
[`near-name-review-2026-11-04.md`](near-name-review-2026-11-04.md)) left 100 same-name pairs and 45
near-name pairs undetermined, and the surname screen
([`surname-change-screen-2026-11-07.md`](surname-change-screen-2026-11-07.md)) found four more
pairs. The web reviewers rated 32 of the same-name groups at 75 per cent or more likely to be one
individual. The earlier reviews had searched ProQuest for 51 same-name groups and 21 near-name
items. This pass searched ProQuest for the rest, starting with the groups rated most likely.

The pass made ten person-record merges (15 links), which remove ten Persons. The release now has
5,068 People. One of the merges rests on the structural rule alone and one, Nunzio Venuto's
Davenport runs, on the structural rule once a Star article removed the namesake that had blocked
it. The other eight rest on newspaper articles.

## Method

On 7 November 2026 the coordinating agent searched ProQuest through Alex Olson's University of
Toronto library sign-in, using the Chrome browser. ProQuest holds the Toronto Star, the
Metroland community papers (Scarborough Mirror, East York Mirror, York Guardian and others) for
some years, the National Post and the Globe and Mail.

The Star printed short profiles of the Toronto candidates for the 1993, 1995 and 1997 elections,
guides to the council and school board candidates for 2003, and a guide to the school board
candidates for 2006. No such profiles were found for the 1999 Ontario election, so runs from that
year were hard to settle. Most searches were for the name, with a party, ward or year to narrow
them. Several guides covered many items at once; the 1995 Ontario profiles of 5 June 1995 helped
settle four groups.

A join needed what the build notes' Identity section requires: a source about the person that
ties the runs, or the structural rules. A shared party alone did not count. The agent read each
article in full in ProQuest and recorded the quoted words with the article's ProQuest document
number. No second agent checked these joins; the earlier reviews used one.

The results are in [`star-pass-2026-11-07/items.csv`](star-pass-2026-11-07/items.csv), one row
per item searched or joined, with the article URLs, the finding and the outcome.

## Searched and not settled

The search found descriptions that fit but do not tie the runs for, among others:

- Gulam Mohamed: a high school math teacher in 1993 and a retired teacher with 40 years in
  education in 2006. A teaching career is not distinctive enough.
- Frank D'Amico: the 2003 TDSB Ward 5 candidate was a 'defence department corporal'; the later
  Catholic trustee is described elsewhere as a Canadian Forces reservist.
- Mike Thomas: the 1995 Conservative was a 21-year-old political science student who had finished
  second in a 1994 council race; the 1997 trustee was a young aide to Councillor Mike Tzekas who ran
  against the same councillor in 2000. Nothing found names both.
- Sam Sotiropoulos, Ellery Hollingsworth, Sean Harrison, Patrick McBrearty, Stuart Weinstein,
  Senai Iman and others: the guides give an occupation or a platform for one run only.

For Don Jackson, Wiktor Borkowski, Kuga Kasilingam, Lionel Poizner and the Mathanalingam pair the
search found result tables, candidate lists or a questionnaire on one run only.

## Changes to the build

A disposition names the Person it confirms or holds against. When a merge deprecates that Person,
the disposition now applies to the Person it was merged into
(`identity_dispositions.apply_identity_review_decisions`). Five dispositions that confirmed
Nunzio Venuto's Davenport runs are replaced by the merges, and their rows in
`data/reference/identity_link_basis.csv` are removed.

The joins came after the census of joins in
[`link-audit-2026-11-07.md`](link-audit-2026-11-07.md) and are not in it.
